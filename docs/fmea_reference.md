# Reference FMEA

A design-stage FMEA for a comparable long-term implantable central venous catheter, used here
as a benchmark for the real-world data. The source is not disclosed. L = likelihood,
S = severity, R = L x S, each on a 1-5 scale.

| # | Failure mode | Failure cause | Consequence | L | S | R | Risk reduction | L | S | R |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Not sterile | Damaged packaging; manufacturing defect | Patient infection | 4 | 5 | 20 | "Do not use if packaging damaged" labelling; QA visual inspection of packaging | 1 | 5 | 5 |
| 2 | Line breaks | Manufacturing defect; wrong material | Piece of line entering circulation, damage to heart and vessels | 3 | 5 | 15 | QA testing; fatigue testing on prototypes | 1 | 5 | 5 |
| 3 | Line rejected by the body | Poor biocompatibility | Inflammatory response | 2 | 5 | 10 | Material choice from literature | 1 | 5 | 5 |
| 4 | Device re-used | Improper training; poor package instructions | Cross-patient infection | 4 | 5 | 20 | Training; single-use marking | 1 | 5 | 5 |
| 5 | Cap or clamp fails | Manufacturing defect; damaged product; past shelf life | Blood loss at flow rate | 4 | 4 | 16 | QA testing; check before use; check expiry | 1 | 4 | 4 |
| 6 | Thrombosis | Patient at risk of thrombosis | Clot in the vessel | 3 | 3 | 9 | Training; anticoagulation if needed | 2 | 3 | 6 |
| 7 | Resistance when drawing or infusing | Occlusion | Damage leading to breakage | 4 | 3 | 12 | Weekly flushing; replace if occluded | 1 | 3 | 3 |
| 8 | Foreign liquid enters line | Patient did not follow instructions | Possible infection | 4 | 4 | 16 | Patient instructions from clinician | 2 | 4 | 8 |
